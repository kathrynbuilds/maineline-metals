## 2. Products and equipment implications

| Product (all confirmed) | Freight implication |
|---|---|
| Hot rolled, cold rolled, pickled & oiled coil | Flatbed with **coil racks/cradles**, chains + edge protection; P&O and CR must be **tarped** (rust/surface-critical) |

## 3. Inbound freight (mill → Mainline)

- **Blytheville sits inside America's densest sheet-steel cluster.** Mississippi County, AR is the top steel-producing county in the U.S.: **Nucor Steel Arkansas (Hickman)** — HR/CR/coated sheet since 1992 plus a $230M cold mill (2019); **Nucor-Yamato** (structural, Blytheville); **U.S. Steel Big River Steel Works (Osceola)** — with a $1.9–2B expansion announced 2025–26 including the country's first DRI facility. Majestic Steel opened a distribution facility on the Nucor Hickman campus in 2024 — evidence this cluster feeds distributor networks. **[Confirmed]** ([Talk Business](https://talkbusiness.net/2019/10/nucor-opens-new-230-million-cold-mill-in-hickman/), [Arkansas Business](https://www.arkansasbusiness.com/article/land-of-steel-expands-osceola-big-river-steel-direct-reduced-iron/), [SMU](https://www.steelmarketupdate.com/2024/05/08/majestic-steel-opens-distribution-facility-in-arkansas/), [Site Selection](https://siteselection.com/forging-ahead-how-arkansas-became-a-global-leader-in-steel-production/))
- **Primary inbound to Mainline Processing [Inference, strong]:** short-haul shuttles from Nucor Hickman (~10–20 mi) and Big River Steel Osceola (~20 mi) — likely dedicated local dray, some by mill-arranged freight. Not a broker opportunity at first, but the *carriers doing it* are exactly the coil-experienced fleets we want.
- **Other inbound [Inference from hub towns]:** Gary IN / Granite City IL / Cleveland OH / Butler IN / Decatur AL map to U.S. Steel Gary Works, U.S. Steel Granite City, Cleveland-Cliffs, Steel Dynamics Butler, and Nucor Decatur — consistent with a distributor buying excess/secondary from many mills. - **Inbound to GSM Acworth [Inference]:** prime coated/painted sheet from Southeastern mills — Nucor Decatur AL (~200 mi), Nucor Berkeley SC, US Steel BRS (via Blytheville) — plus intercompany transfers from Mainline Processing. The anchor lane below *is partly their own intercompany freight*.

## 6. Load characteristics

- **Coil weights:** Mainline's own slitters handle master coils to **50,000 lb**; road-legal gross limits mean typical shipped coils/loads run **40,000–48,000 lb** (single master coil or multiple slit coils totaling ≤ ~48k on a 5-axle). **[Confirmed equipment spec; standard industry practice]**
- **Securement:** 49 CFR **393.120** applies to any coil(s) ≥ 5,000 lb — eyes-crosswise coils must ride in cradles/chocks/timbers secured against rolling (nailed blocking alone prohibited); eyes-vertical requires tiedowns against tipping fore/aft/lateral. Carriers need coil racks, chains, edge protection, and demonstrated coil experience — this is the freight class where securement failures kill people, and shippers audit for it. **[Confirmed]** ([eCFR 393.120](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-393/subpart-I/subject-group-ECFR4edbddd531bf160/section-393.120), [FMCSA cargo securement rules](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/regulations/cargo-securement/cargo-securement-rules))
- **Tarping:** not universally required by regulation — it's commodity/shipper-driven. For Mainline's mix (P&O, CR, coated, **pre-painted**) assume tarps or Conestoga on nearly everything; painted product is claim-prone. **[Confirmed reg posture; inference on Mainline's requirement]**
- **Permits:** standard loads are legal-weight/legal-dimension; no routine oversize/overweight permits expected. (Exception: rare 50k+ master-coil moves would need overweight permits — treat as out of scope.) **[Inference]**
